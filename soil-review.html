Loading the live page and waiting for measured data…

This sheet is live. It loads the real trail page in the background and calls the real scoring model, so every number below is today's, not a snapshot someone pasted in. Nothing to regenerate — reload it and it is current. The catch is the same one the page has: wait for it to finish. Cards paint from forecast first and measured data seconds later, and the numbers move hard. The status bar above turns green when the measured payload has landed.

Exposure moves the score less than I used to claim — but it moves the timing a lot. This sheet and CLAUDE.md both used to say exposure hit about as hard as soil. Measured against live data on the evening of 5 Aug 2026, that was wrong. Swinging a trail all the way from shaded to exposed moved today's score by 0–14 points depending on the trail, where the soil relabel had moved Bent Creek 19 points and Pisgah Lower 28.

What it does move is when a trail comes back. On the same run, Pisgah Lower came good 26 hours earlier as exposed than as shaded; Pisgah Upper 21 hours; Bent Creek recovered inside the forecast window as exposed and never as shaded. That follows from the mechanics — exposure scales the drying rate, so it needs dry hours to compound and does almost nothing to a score taken while it is still raining. Watch the "comes good" line, not just the number.
