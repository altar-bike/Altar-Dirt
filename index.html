Missing somewhere?

Tell us where you ride and we'll add it. The nearest town is enough — coordinates help, and so does anything you know about how the dirt behaves after rain.

This is a model, not a trail report. Soil moisture and rainfall come from forecast data, not from someone standing on the trail. If a trail is closed, gated, or it rained harder than the model thinks, the model is wrong and your eyes are right. Check the land manager before you drive.
